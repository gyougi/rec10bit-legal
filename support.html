Frequently asked questions

The app says “Permission error” / nothing records

Rec10bit needs Screen Recording permission. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, enable Rec10bit, then quit and relaunch the app.

How do I record a specific window or area?

Pick a mode at the top (Area / Window / Full screen). For Area, drag a rectangle and press Enter. For Window, click “Pick Window”, hover the target window, and press Enter. Recording targets the display Rec10bit’s own window is on — move the app to that display first.

Where are my recordings saved?

To the output folder you choose with the “Browse” button. Files are named Rec10bit-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.mp4 (video) or .heic / .png (stills).

A thin white line appears at the edge in QuickTime

That’s a QuickTime rendering quirk with wide-gamut 10-bit HEVC; the file itself is fine and the line does not appear in VLC, Final Cut Pro, or after uploading to services like YouTube.
