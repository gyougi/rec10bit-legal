English

Rec10bit PRO (“the App”) is a screen recording and screenshot utility for macOS, developed by Gyou.Gi (“we”, “us”). Rec10bit PRO does its job entirely on your own Mac. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Rec10bit PRO does not collect any personal information, analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, or device identifiers. The App has no account system and requires no sign-in.

Your recordings and screenshots

  • All video recordings and screenshots are written only to the output folder you choose, on your own Mac.
  • We never see, upload, or transmit your recordings or screenshots. They never leave your device through the App.
  • The App contains no networking code for sending your content anywhere.

Purchases & subscriptions

  • Rec10bit PRO offers a 7-day free trial and an auto-renewable monthly subscription. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit).
  • We never receive or store your name, payment card, or any billing information — Apple handles all billing.
  • We operate no server. Your subscription status is verified on your device through Apple's StoreKit, and the free-trial start date is stored only in your local macOS Keychain. None of this is sent to us.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording): required by macOS for the App to capture your screen and the audio of other apps. Used solely to perform the recording you request; captured frames and audio are encoded directly to the local file you specify.
  • Folder access: the App writes only to the folder you explicitly select via the system “Choose folder” dialog.

Third parties

Rec10bit PRO embeds no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It is built only with Apple’s own system frameworks (ScreenCaptureKit, AVFoundation, StoreKit, AppKit, SwiftUI).

Children

Rec10bit PRO does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact: gyou.gi@glspace.co.jp
